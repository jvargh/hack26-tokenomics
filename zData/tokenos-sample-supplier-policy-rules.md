# Northstar Logistics Supplier Freight Policy

**Policy ID:** NLS-FRT-2026-02

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

**Applies to:** Freight and delivery services charged to Logistics Operations purchase orders

## 1. Standard freight services

Standard parcel, ground, and contracted two-day delivery charges are payable when they are tied to an active purchase order and a shipment reference.

## 2. Fuel surcharge

A fuel surcharge is payable when all of the following are true:

1. The charge is calculated as a percentage of eligible transport charges.
2. The percentage does not exceed the published monthly cap of **8.0%**.
3. The invoice identifies the period or fuel index used.

## 3. Weekend and expedited delivery

Weekend or expedited delivery charges are payable only when the invoice identifies a valid approval ID issued before dispatch. An approval ID is not required when a documented safety or medical emergency is attached to the shipment record.

## 4. Address correction fees

Address correction fees are payable when the supplier identifies the shipment reference and the correction was caused by an ordering or destination-data error. No additional management approval is required.

## 5. Peak season capacity surcharge

Peak season or network-capacity surcharges are not payable unless all of the following are attached:

1. A written exception approval from Logistics Operations.
2. The approval date.
3. A stated maximum approved amount.

## 6. Duplicate charges

A charge must be rejected when it repeats the same supplier, shipment or batch reference, charge type, and amount as a previously invoiced line for the same service period, unless the supplier provides evidence of a separate service event.

## 7. Customs documentation support

Customs documentation support is payable when it is tied to an import record and the active purchase order permits international freight support.

## 8. Required decision evidence

Every approved or rejected charge in a review summary must cite:

- Invoice line number
- Policy section
- Shipment, batch, or import reference
- Approval ID, where applicable

## 9. Review outcome categories

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Approve | All policy requirements and evidence are present. |
| Request evidence | The charge may be allowable, but required evidence or approval is missing. |
| Reject | The charge violates a clear rule, such as duplication or a missing required exception. |
